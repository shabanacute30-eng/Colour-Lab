Privacy Policy
Colour Lab • Last Updated 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to Colour Lab. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you play our game.

2 Information We Collect

Colour Lab is designed to be a safe and family-friendly game. We collect minimal data:

  • Game Progress: Your level completions and best scores are stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the game experience.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.

3 How We Use Your Information

Any information we collect is used solely to:

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Improve gameplay balance and difficulty
  • Fix bugs and enhance performance

4 Data Storage & Security

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your progress to external servers. Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

5 Third-Party Services

Colour Lab does not share your information with third parties. We do not include advertisements or trackers that collect personal information.

6 Children's Privacy

Colour Lab is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Reset your game progress at any time
  • Uninstall the game and remove all local data
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our practices, please reach out through the game's support option.

Thank you for playing!

About Colour Lab
Colour Lab is a premium water sort puzzle game designed to challenge your mind while keeping you relaxed. With over 500 hand-crafted and dynamically generated levels, every stage offers a fresh puzzle that is guaranteed to be solvable — no impossible levels, only pure satisfaction.
The game combines elegant visuals with smooth liquid physics, satisfying pour animations, and a luxurious green-themed UI. Each level gradually increases in difficulty, keeping the experience engaging from Level 1 to Level 500.
